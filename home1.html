— Our Story & Heritage —

Five Generations of Uncompromising Affinage

Founded in 1924 along high-altitude alpine pastures, Cheesoria began with a singular obsession: preserving the sacred art of slow-matured, raw pasture milk cheese.

Every wheel is carefully hand-turned, washed with reserve Cabernet Sauvignon, and left to slumber in subterranean sandstone cellars. Here, time and nature weave complex protein crystals, velvety rinds, and unforgettable aromas.

— The Cheesoria Difference —

How Master Cheese Is Made

From raw alpine pasture milking to 36-month subterranean cave maturation, discover the 4 sacred stages of authentic affineur cheesemaking.

01 Phase 1: Pure Milk

Raw Alpine Herd Milking

Single-origin unpasteurized milk gathered daily at dawn from heritage grass-fed cows grazing on wild mountain herbs for pure taste.

02 Phase 2: Hand Curdling

Copper Cauldron Curd Cutting

Milk is gently warmed over wood-fire in copper cauldrons and hand-cut with harp wires into soft curd pearls for fantastic texture.

03 Phase 3: Wine Bathing

Wine & Sea-Salt Hand Rubbing

Pressed curds are submerged in estate Cabernet Sauvignon and hand-rubbed with Mediterranean sea salt for perfect rind crusting.

04 Phase 4: Cave Maturation

36M Subterranean Affinage Vault

Wheels slumber inside natural subterranean limestone cellars, hand-turned weekly by master affineurs to develop rich crunchy crystals.
