80-Foot Subterranean Limestone Aging Caverns in the Pre-Alps

Key Affineur Insights & Takeaways

  • Subterranean cavern aging provides a constant 11°C (52°F) environment and 92% relative humidity essential for crystalline development.
  • Tyrosine crystals form naturally when enzymes break down milk protein chains during 24 to 36 months of cellar maturation.
  • Optimal sommelier pairing requires high-acid, oxidative Jura whites or aged tawny ports to cut through deep, nutty butterfat.
