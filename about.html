— Our Heritage & Journey —

A Century of Uncompromising Excellence

From a single subterranean cavern in 1924 to a globally revered benchmark in artisanal cheese.

1924

The Alpine Cavern Beginnings

Founded by Master Affineur Laurent Vance in the limestone caves of the French Pre-Alps. Laurent discovered that 11°C ambient air and 92% humidity yielded unprecedented flavor complexity in raw milk wheels.

1958

Heirloom Herd Stewardship

Partnering exclusively with traditional family pastures in Normandy and Reggio Emilia. Preserving rare Montbéliarde and Reggiana Red Cow herds rich in K-casein AA proteins.

1989

Subterranean Vault Expansion

Expanding aging capacity into 80-foot underground natural stone vaults filtered by porous calcite fissures, fostering natural Penicillium spores without artificial climate control.

2015

World Cheese Supreme Champion

Cheesoria’s 36-Month Vintage Gouda Reserve was crowned Supreme Champion at the International World Cheese Awards, recognized for its crystalline tyrosine crunch and butterscotch finishing notes.

Today

Modern Gourmet Commerce

Bringing century-old cellar traditions directly to gourmets nationwide. Delivering temperature-controlled raw milk masterworks, sommelier pairing guides, and luxury gift collections.

— The 4-Step Protocol —

The Art & Science of Cheese Creation

Combining ancient biological fermentation with meticulous cellar guardianship.

01

Pure Heirloom Milk Sourcing

Harvested daily at dawn from pasture-grazed heirloom herds. Unpasteurized raw milk retains natural flora, essential minerals, and rich beta-carotene.

100% Unpasteurized
02

Copper Cauldron Stirring

Curds are heated slowly inside traditional copper cauldrons and cut by hand using classic harp knives to preserve maximum curd tenderness.

Copper Kettle Fermentation
03

Subterranean Cave Maturation

Wheels mature on organic pine boards inside 11°C limestone caves for 12 to 36 months to develop savory crystalline tyrosine crunch.

92% Humidity Caverns
04

Acoustic Hammer & Wax Seal

Wheels are sound-tested with silver mallets by master affineurs to verify interior density and moisture before official gold stamping.

AOP Certified Grade
